GraalVM Community Mascot

The GraalVM mascot is an open-source creation. This means that our broad community of contributors, teams, and other enthusiasts are welcome to use the mascot in any context. On this page, you will find files corresponding to all illustrations of the mascot. The library will be regularly updated, so we encourage you to visit it periodically for new additions to the mascot's story.
